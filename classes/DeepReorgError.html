    Class DeepReorgError

    Thrown by sync() when a reorg's fork point cannot be found: it is deeper than MAX_REORG_DEPTH (or blockHashRetention), or the walk reached a height whose hash the wallet no longer stores. The wallet is left as it was. Recover with resetSyncState() on the TransactionKeysSync (client.getSyncManager()) and a fresh sync.
